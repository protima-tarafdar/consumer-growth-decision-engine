# Business problem

**Question.** How can a consumer business find the customers with the greatest growth opportunity, and decide which intervention creates incremental behavior?

- **Outcome:** purchase within 30 days (re-engagement / frequency).
- **Segments:** New, Active, At Risk, Lapsed (recency and frequency rules).
- **Experiment:** control = no offer; treatment = $5 off $30.
- **Core ML question:** who is most likely to purchase *because of* the intervention?
- **Method:** uplift / incremental-response modeling (two-model baseline).
- **Metrics:** conversion, incremental lift, incremental purchases, incremental value, intervention cost, return per discount dollar.
- **Decision:** who to target, who not to target, what offer they get, and whether it pays.
